# LightFlow 1.6.5

The targeted ART action and read-only status report now include Messenger (`com.facebook.orca`) alongside Facebook. Compilation remains opt-in, thermal-gated, and limited to Android's existing `speed-profile` artifacts.

No LSPosed hook, resident daemon, CPU boost, refresh-rate lock, or RAM cleaner was added. LightFlow remains a boot-time Magisk policy with no background polling.
